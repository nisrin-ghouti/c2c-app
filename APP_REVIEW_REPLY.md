**3. FUNCTIONS, TARGET AUDIENCE, PROBLEM, AND VALUE**

C2C is a community-engagement app for verified university and college students aged 16 or older. Its initial launch serves Tel Aviv University students. It solves the problem of events and opportunities being scattered across university channels and difficult to discover. C2C brings them together in one place. Students can join events/programs, connect and chat with peers, expand their social and professional networks, arrange meetings, and participate more actively in their community.

C2C is completely free for students, participating businesses, and all other users. Students never purchase points. Points are awarded only for eligible free participation, such as attending an event or completing a verified student meeting. Businesses voluntarily provide complimentary benefits that students may redeem using earned points.

Points are promotional rewards with no monetary value. They cannot be bought, sold, transferred, traded, withdrawn, or converted into cash. Redeeming a benefit is not a purchase: the app displays the benefit and validates redemption through a code/QR process only. C2C does not sell products or services, process payments, or transfer money, and charges no fees or commissions to anyone. The app contains no in-app purchases, subscriptions, paid content or features, external purchase links, payment flows, or payment processors.

**4. SETUP AND ACCESS**

Registration requires a @mail.tau.ac.il student email and email verification. Users can sign in with email and password or with a one-time code sent by email. For review, use the pre-registered, verified credentials provided in App Review Information and select "Sign in with password." This does not require access to the university inbox or a verification code. Main features are available from the Home screen and bottom navigation. No sample files are required. Account deletion is available from the Profile screen.

**5. EXTERNAL SERVICES**

- **Supabase** — authentication and backend database/storage.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google)** — delivers push notifications, which are then passed to Apple Push Notification service for delivery to the device.
- **Apple Push Notification service** — device-level notification delivery.
- **Firebase Hosting (Google)** — hosts the benefit-redemption confirmation page that a participating business opens when scanning a student's redemption QR code.
- **Google Cloud Translation API** — translates event and benefit text supplied by campus organizers into the app's three supported languages. Only organizer-supplied event and benefit content is sent; no personal or student data is included.
- **Device camera** — QR scanning, after the user grants permission.

No payment processor, financial platform, or paid-content provider is used.

**6. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES**

The app works consistently for all eligible users and does not change based on region. Access depends on student eligibility, not location. Events and benefits reflect participating campus organizations and local businesses. The interface is available in Arabic, Hebrew, and English.
